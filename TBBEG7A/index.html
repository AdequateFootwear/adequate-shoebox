Your mission

Archaeopteryx is one of the earliest known birds, with feathers but also teeth and a long bony tail. Do an earthcache and photograph a bird you see there, or a feather you find along the way (left where it lies). Identify the EC and provide a photo!

Its goal

To stay in AdequateFootwear's inventory as a relic from times past!

About

An Archaeopteryx fossil tag. Archaeopteryx lived about 150 million years ago in what is now southern Germany, where its fossils are preserved in fine-grained Solnhofen limestone. With feathers, teeth, clawed fingers, and a long bony tail, it is one of the earliest known birds and a famous link between dinosaurs and birds.
